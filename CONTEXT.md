# MIS Frontend Domain Glossary

Municipal Information System (MIS) data collection and household survey management.

## Language

**Household Profile**:
A comprehensive multi-section survey recording a family's details, including residence, members, economic, health, agriculture, livestock, decision making, and disaster preparedness.

**Survey Draft**:
An in-progress household survey stored locally before final submission.

**Case**:
A data collection survey instance tracking household profile trees and form draft progress.
